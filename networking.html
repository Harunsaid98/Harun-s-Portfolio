Lab 03 · Switching

VLAN access ports

I connected three PCs to one switch, placed two devices in VLAN 10 and one in VLAN 20, and tested which hosts could communicate. All three PCs deliberately used the same 192.168.1.0/24 subnet so the test focused on VLAN separation.

PC0 + PC1VLAN 10 · STAFF
—
Layer 2 switchAccess ports
—
PC2VLAN 20 · MANAGEMENT
Configured

Created VLANs 10 and 20, assigned access ports and saved the switch configuration.

Tested

PC0 could ping PC1 in the same VLAN, but could not reach PC2 in the other VLAN.

Takeaway

A Layer 2 switch separates broadcast domains even when devices share an IP subnet.

Key verification commandsshow vlan briefping 192.168.1.20ping 192.168.1.30

Lab 04 · Switching

VLAN trunk between switches

I linked two switches with an 802.1Q trunk so VLAN 10 and VLAN 20 could travel across one physical connection. PCs in the same VLAN communicated across the switches, while traffic stayed separated between VLANs.

SW0VLANs 10 & 20
⟷
802.1Q trunkGi0/1 · allowed 10,20
⟷
SW1VLANs 10 & 20
Configured

Made Gi0/1 a trunk and allowed VLANs 10 and 20 between switches.

Verified

Same-VLAN PCs on different switches communicated successfully; cross-VLAN traffic remained blocked without routing.

Takeaway

A trunk carries multiple VLANs over one link, but does not provide inter-VLAN routing.

Key verification commandsshow vlan briefshow interfaces trunk

Lab 06 · Routing

Static routing between two LANs

I connected two separate networks through two routers. Each router could already reach its local network, so I added static routes for the network on the other side.

LAN A192.168.1.0/24
—
R0 ↔ R110.0.0.0/30
—
LAN B192.168.2.0/24
Configured

Added a route to the remote LAN on each router using the neighboring router as the next hop.

Verified

Checked show ip route and ping tests. PCs on opposite sides could communicate once routes were added.

Takeaway

A router needs a route for networks that are not directly connected.

Static route examplesip route 192.168.2.0 255.255.255.0 10.0.0.2ip route 192.168.1.0 255.255.255.0 10.0.0.1

DHCP per VLAN
